Aircraft Rental Agreement

Fire Hound Rental

This agreement is between Fire Hound Rental, hereinafter referred to as the “Operator,” and the “Renter” identified in the signature section of this agreement regarding the use of the designated aircraft hereinafter referred to as the “aircraft.”

A.

(1) Renter acknowledges and agrees that the aircraft is the property of the Operator.

(2) Renter acknowledges that he has inspected the aircraft and has found it to be in good mechanical condition and airworthy. Renter has inspected the aircraft for any damage and noted such damage on the renter invoice.

(3) Renter agrees to return the aircraft at the scheduled time, weather permitting.

(4) Renter agrees to properly secure the aircraft in its hangar after each flight.

B.

Renter expressly acknowledges personal liability to pay Operator on demand or immediately at the completion of the rental period:

(1) Service and time charges computed at the applicable posted rates until said aircraft is returned to its hangar and rental period is completed. Acceptable forms of payment are cash or check only.

(2) Any loss or damage to the aircraft, its components, parts or equipment during the rental period will be paid for by the renter or the renter’s insurance. If the damage results in the aircraft being removed from service, the renter agrees to pay the operator for two hours per day at the current hourly rental rates until said aircraft is returned to airworthy condition.

(3) The hangar is under 24 hour video surveillance. Any damage to the aircraft that is not reported will cause the renter to lose flight privileges.

C.

Renter agrees not to tamper with, molest, or attempt to repair any parts of the aircraft or its accessories, but will contact the Operator for instructions upon encountering mechanical malfunctions or any damage to the aircraft.

D.

Renter agrees to report any aircraft damage, accident or incident to the Operator at the completion of rental period or as soon as possible.

E.

Renter agrees that rented aircraft shall not be used or operated:

(1) For any illegal purposes.

(2) In any race, speed test, or contest.

(3) By any person other than the Renter who signed the agreement.

(4) Outside the limits of the Continental United States.

(5) To carry passengers or property for compensation or hire.

(6) For any flight for which the Renter is not properly rated or certified.

F.

Renter agrees to sign and comply with future renter guides provided by Fire Hound Rental.

G. Cancellation Policy.

Reservations cancelled less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the scheduled rental start time are subject to a $50 cancellation fee. Reservations cancelled twenty-four (24) hours or more prior to the scheduled rental start time are not subject to a cancellation fee. Cancellations due to weather are exempt from this fee.

H.

Renter, by affixing his signature hereon, does agree to follow the following flight operations safety rules, emergency maintenance procedures and insurance provisions.

Flight Operations Safety Rules

  • Pilot Certificate – Renter must hold a valid and current pilot certificate and/or student pilot certificate with appropriate ratings. The Renter will fly the aircraft from the left seat, and he/she will be responsible for the aircraft and its operation at all times.
  • Currency – Renter must possess evidence of a current biennial flight review, medical certificate, and aircraft checkout by a pilot designated by the Operator.
  • Preflight – Renter will personally conduct a preflight inspection of the aircraft as prescribed by the manufacturer. Renter shall use the manufacturer’s recommended pre-takeoff, cruise and pre-landing checklists.
  • Weather – Renter shall plan to operate the aircraft only when the present and forecasted weather indicates VFR conditions local and in route (ceiling of at least 3,000 feet and visibility 5 miles or greater) unless otherwise approved by CFI or Renter is instrument rated, current for IFR.
  • Take-off and landing area – No take-off or landing shall be made on any area other than the runways of an airport designed, constructed, maintained, and used as an airport with runways of no less than 2,000 feet. Runway lengths shorter than 2,000 feet must be approved by CFI.
  • Physical conditions – Renter shall not operate the aircraft for a period of at least 12 hours after using any intoxicating substances such as liquor, tranquilizers, and sleeping aids.
  • The Renter will comply with all local, state and federal regulations.

Transient Maintenance Policy

  • In the event of a breakdown, notify the Operator of the problem immediately. If maintenance personnel are not available leave your name, aircraft number and telephone number where you can be contacted.
  • Do not authorize any repairs to be made to the aircraft without clearance from the Operator representative. Failure to do so could result in the Renter being responsible for a portion or the entirety of the bill.
  • The Operator will reimburse the Renter for fuel purchased, provided that the Renter presents a fuel receipt from the place of purchase that shows the number of gallons, and price purchased.
  • All repairs and fueling will be made by properly licensed facilities and personnel.

Notice of Insurance Coverage

Renters are required to purchase an “Aircraft Renters Insurance Policy” of a minimum value of $75,000.00 from an independent insurance company to protect themselves in situations where they may be found to be negligent or responsible for damages. In the event the repairs exceed the renter’s insurance policy value or the aircraft replacement, the renter agrees to cover any costs not covered by the renter’s insurance policy. The safety of any passengers or persons present in the vicinity of the aircraft or in the hangar other than renter will be the responsibility of the renter. Fire Hound Rental reserves the right to increase the value of Renters Insurance required at will.
